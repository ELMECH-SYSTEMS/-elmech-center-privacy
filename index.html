Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how ELMECH SYSTEMS G.P. processes information in connection with the Elmech Center mobile application.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Internal business application. Elmech Center is designed for authorized personnel and other authorized users of ELMECH SYSTEMS G.P. and is not intended for general consumer use.

1. Purpose of Elmech Center

ELMECH SYSTEMS G.P. (“ELMECH SYSTEMS”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) provides the Elmech Center mobile application to support authorized company operations.

Elmech Center provides authorized users with secure mobile access to business systems and workflows including customer relationship management (CRM), customers, products, service management, equipment, maintenance contracts, inspections, activities, notifications, and related company operations.

Access to production functionality is restricted to authorized users and authorized devices.

2. Information Processed by the Application

Depending on the user's role and the functionality being used, Elmech Center may process information including:

  • User identity and account information, such as a user's name, account identity, and assigned company role.
  • Business contact information contained within authorized company records.
  • Customer, CRM, product, equipment, installation, maintenance-contract, inspection, service, and activity information required for company operations.
  • Content submitted through the application, including notes, photographs, signatures, inspection information, attachments, and other business records.
  • Device and installation identifiers used to identify and authorize an installation of Elmech Center.
  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device tokens and related technical information required to deliver application notifications.
  • Application version, operating-system version, device model, language, and similar technical information required for application functionality, compatibility, security, and support.

Elmech Center processes this information only as required to provide its business functionality, maintain security, and support authorized company operations.

3. Device Enrollment and Secure Access

Production access to Elmech Center is protected by a company-managed device enrollment process.

The application may generate and store technical identifiers necessary to recognize an authorized installation and establish secure access to ELMECH SYSTEMS infrastructure.

Elmech Center includes an integrated secure networking mechanism for accessing protected company services. This connection is used for application functionality and security and is not used for advertising, tracking, or profiling.

Authentication information and security credentials required by the application are protected using security mechanisms provided by iOS, including the Apple Keychain where applicable.

4. Push Notifications

Elmech Center uses Apple Push Notification service (APNs) to provide authorized users with business-related notifications.

For this purpose, the application registers an APNs device token together with information required to associate the installation with the appropriate authorized user or device.

Push-notification information is used only to provide and manage Elmech Center notifications.

5. Camera, Photos, Files, and Face ID

Certain Elmech Center functionality may allow or require authorized users to:

  • Use the camera for supported business workflows, including barcode scanning and capturing relevant business images.
  • Select photographs or files for authorized business records.
  • Provide signatures where required by a service or inspection workflow.
  • Use Face ID to facilitate secure access to the application.

Access to these device capabilities is subject to the permissions and controls provided by iOS.

Elmech Center does not receive or store the user's Face ID biometric data. Face ID authentication is performed by Apple's operating system.

6. How Information Is Used

Information processed through Elmech Center is used for purposes including:

  • Providing application functionality.
  • Authenticating users and authorizing devices.
  • Providing CRM and service-management functionality.
  • Managing equipment, installations, contracts, inspections, activities, and related business records.
  • Delivering business notifications.
  • Maintaining application and information security.
  • Diagnosing technical issues and supporting authorized users.
  • Maintaining the integrity and availability of ELMECH SYSTEMS business systems.

Information collected through Elmech Center is not used for third-party advertising or advertising measurement.

7. Tracking and Advertising

Elmech Center does not use personal information for cross-app or cross-website advertising tracking.

Elmech Center does not display third-party advertising.

ELMECH SYSTEMS does not sell information collected through Elmech Center to data brokers or advertisers.

8. App Demo

Elmech Center contains an App Demo environment intended to allow Apple App Review and other authorized demonstrations of the application's functionality without providing access to confidential company systems.

App Demo uses fictional demonstration information and does not require access to ELMECH SYSTEMS production infrastructure.

App Demo is not intended to expose production company or customer information.

9. Data Sharing and Service Providers

Information processed through Elmech Center may be transmitted to systems and service providers necessary to provide the application's functionality, secure communications, notifications, hosting, or related technical services.

Such processing is limited to what is necessary to operate and secure the application and ELMECH SYSTEMS business infrastructure.

ELMECH SYSTEMS does not share information collected through Elmech Center with third parties for their independent advertising or marketing purposes.

10. Data Retention

Business information accessed or submitted through Elmech Center is retained according to ELMECH SYSTEMS operational, contractual, legal, security, and record-retention requirements.

Technical device-registration information may be retained for as long as necessary to maintain or audit authorized application access and may be deactivated or removed when access is revoked or no longer required.

11. Security

ELMECH SYSTEMS uses administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information processed through Elmech Center.

These safeguards include authenticated access, device authorization, encrypted communications, secure credential storage, restricted access to internal systems, and the ability to revoke authorized devices.

No electronic system can guarantee absolute security, but ELMECH SYSTEMS takes reasonable measures to protect information processed by the application.

12. User Rights and Requests

Authorized users may contact ELMECH SYSTEMS regarding questions or requests concerning personal information processed through Elmech Center.

Where applicable, requests concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other rights relating to personal data will be handled in accordance with applicable data-protection legislation, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Certain information may need to be retained where required for legitimate business, contractual, security, or legal purposes.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Elmech Center's functionality, data-processing practices, or applicable legal requirements change.

The current version will remain publicly available at this address.
